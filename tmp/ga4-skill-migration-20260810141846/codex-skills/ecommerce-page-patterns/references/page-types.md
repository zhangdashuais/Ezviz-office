# Page Types / 页面类型

## Product Detail Page / 产品详情页

Goal: make the buyer understand what the product does, why it is better, and whether it fits their use case.

目标：让用户快速理解产品解决什么问题、比普通产品强在哪里、是否适合自己的场景。

Recommended order:

1. Hero: product render/lifestyle scene, product name, short promise, 4-6 benefit bullets, primary CTA.
2. Quick intro: visual cards for the strongest features.
3. Signature feature story: one feature per section, with visual proof.
4. Use-case scenes: porch, driveway, apartment, family, package delivery, night, bad weather.
5. App/control interaction: alerts, live view, two-way talk, privacy zones, detection zones.
6. Trust: privacy, local/cloud storage, warranty, support, reviews, certifications.
7. Specs: scannable table, not a wall of text.
8. Comparison: help the user choose between similar models.
9. FAQ and support links.

Interaction patterns:

- Sticky subnav: Overview, Specs, Accessories, FAQ, Reviews.
- Sticky buy bar or CTA on scroll.
- Before/after slider for night vision, WDR, distortion correction, zoom.
- Tabbed feature demos for app functions.
- Accordion specs and FAQs on mobile.
- Product gallery with detail callouts.

## Promotion Campaign Page / 促销活动页

Goal: drive fast selection and checkout while keeping deal rules clear.

目标：让用户快速找到合适优惠并下单，同时看清活动规则。

Recommended order:

1. Campaign hero: event name, discount range, deadline, coupon code, CTA.
2. Category anchor nav: doorbells, cameras, bundles, accessories, best sellers.
3. Deal cards: product image, discount, final price, code/copy button, Buy Now, Learn More.
4. Bundle logic: starter kit, whole-home kit, upgrade kit.
5. Social proof or editor picks.
6. Rules: validity, exclusions, shipping, return policy.

Interaction patterns:

- Coupon copy button with visible state.
- Category filters and anchor jumps.
- Countdown only when the campaign truly has a deadline.
- Price comparison: original price, sale price, saved amount.
- Mobile sticky campaign CTA or category switcher.

## Topic Information / Product Family Page

Goal: explain one shared technology, use case, solution, or product family before helping visitors select among related products. Product-level purchase buttons support the information journey but do not lead it.

Recommended order:

1. Topic hero: family/technology name, shared promise, 3-4 proof points, and a family or scenario visual.
2. Sticky topic navigation: Overview, Technology, Series, Compare, FAQ.
3. Shared technology story: a visual explanation of the common platform or use case.
4. Series segmentation: group products by user need, environment, coverage, or performance tier.
5. Series cards: who each series is for, decisive capabilities, included models, and Learn More / Compare actions.
6. Product selection: recommendation cards followed by a comparison matrix.
7. FAQ and model-level next actions: product detail, compatibility, or Where to Buy.

Interaction patterns:

- Tabs, hotspots, or short scrollytelling for abstract shared technology.
- Sticky topic subnav with active section state.
- Need-based series selector rather than a raw SKU grid.
- Comparison table with a desktop sticky first column and mobile product selector/accordions.
- Track topic progression separately from purchase intent: technology view, series selection, comparison use, product selection, outbound action.

## Landing Page / 落地页

Goal: convert one audience around one offer, product launch, or campaign message.

目标：围绕一个人群、一个卖点或一个活动完成转化。

Recommended order:

1. Hero: one audience, one promise, one visual signal.
2. Pain-to-benefit section: show the problem and the product outcome.
3. Proof modules: feature demos, specs, reviews, before/after, app screenshots.
4. Offer or lead capture.
5. Objection handling: privacy, battery life, setup, compatibility, storage, weather.
6. Final CTA.

Interaction patterns:

- Scroll-based feature reveal only when it clarifies the product.
- Compact lead form or CTA.
- Comparison strip against old solution or competitor category.
- Responsive sections that preserve the same narrative on mobile.
